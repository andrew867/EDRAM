The [XA-Core disk recovery](xacore/README.md) preserves the EDRAM firmware, announcement loads and disk record wrappers found in three archived XA-Core images.

File hashes and extraction findings are in ../VALIDATION.json. The original source archive is downloadable from its upstream URL; its redundant preexisting WAV and A-law exports are not duplicated here.
